Meet Denys

Denys was born on 10 September 2019. He is now seven years old, and his family is asking for support with the cost of his treatment.

At home, he can spend hours playing with his favourite toy cars and inventing new adventures for them. He loves riding his bicycle, feeling the breeze, and enjoying every moment of movement. Denys is always excited to go to the playground because he enjoys spending time and socialising with other children. Sadly, his condition significantly limits his physical abilities, preventing him from joining every game on equal terms with his peers.

Treatment

Why this fundraiser is needed

A letter from Fakeeh University Hospital dated 24 August 2026 confirms Denys’s diagnosis of Duchenne muscular dystrophy.

After reviewing the genetic reports and the negative Anti-AAVrh74 test result, Dr Arif Khan stated that Denys was clinically eligible for Elevidys gene therapy.

The preliminary cost of the treatment package is approximately USD 2.9 million. The family has launched this fundraiser, and donations can be made through Monobank, Privat24, PayPal, or the charity foundation’s account.

This information is based on the letter and preliminary invoice issued by Fakeeh University Hospital on 24 August 2026. The final treatment decision rests with the medical team.

Elevidys gene therapy · Dubai, UAE

A chance to receive the treatment he needs

The invoice includes the medicine and infusion, specialist consultations, pre- and post-treatment tests, up to two nights in intensive care, and up to 24 physiotherapy sessions. The hospital requires a minimum stay of 75 days, while the package includes family accommodation for up to 88 days.
